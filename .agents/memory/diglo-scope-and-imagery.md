Diglo's main services are Gift Packs, Corporate Uniforms, PPE, and Printing Services. Do not add Social Media as a service.

Only use exact, free-to-use images for products and industries. Do not use paid stock, paid image search, or image generation that charges credits. If no exact free image is available, keep a clear photo-pending placeholder until the user provides an approved image.

**Why:** the user explicitly locked the service list and required free-only imagery with no charges.

**How to apply:** Keep the four main services unchanged; treat industry/product items as subsections. Verify image subject matches its card or project before displaying it.
